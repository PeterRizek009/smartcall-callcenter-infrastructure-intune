# Operations & Lifecycle

## Offboarding (Employee leaves)
- BYOD: Retire (remove corporate data only)
- Corporate device: Wipe (factory reset)
- After wipe (optional): Delete device object for portal cleanup

## Lost Device
1) Wipe immediately
2) Revoke sessions (Entra ID)
3) Optional: Delete device entry after confirmation

## Reassignment
- Wipe device
- Autopilot will re-provision at next boot
